# Medical research

Decompose the medical question into population, intervention, comparator,
outcome, safety, study design, and time horizon. Search PubMed for clinical
literature and ClinicalTrials.gov for registered or ongoing studies. Separate
peer-reviewed results from registry status and unpublished findings. Preserve
sample sizes, effect measures, confidence intervals, adverse events, study
phase, and population limits. State uncertainty and never turn research
evidence into individualized medical advice.
