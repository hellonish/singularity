# Financial research

Anchor company claims in SEC filings and distinguish reported facts from
management guidance, analyst interpretation, and market commentary. Normalize
fiscal periods, currencies, units, and GAAP versus non-GAAP measures before
comparison. Cover growth drivers, demand, competition, supply constraints,
capital structure, and regulatory risks when relevant. Cite the exact filing
period and retain contradictory or qualified disclosures. Do not present the
result as personalized investment advice.
